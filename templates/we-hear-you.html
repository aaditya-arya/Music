What should I include in a complaint or appeal?+

Include the report or project reference, manufacturer or vendor, the relevant events and the outcome you are seeking. If you do not have a report reference yet, use the project name.

How does certificate verification work?+

Select certificate verification and provide the document number, document type and issue date if known. You can attach a copy for context. Our team reviews the request; the form does not verify documents instantly.

Can I send a request before my scope is final?+

Yes. Choose quick inquiry and share your equipment, location and current project stage. Select “Other / Not sure yet” if you need help identifying the service.

How do I apply for a role at AES?+

Visit Careers at AES to explore openings and submit your CV through the application form.

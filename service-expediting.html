About Us Service Careers We hear you

Supply chain bottlenecks and unmonitored vendor slippages are the primary causes of massive EPC project delays and cost overruns. Akshar Engineering Services provides proactive on-site expediting services, placing experienced expeditors directly on supplier and sub-supplier shop floors worldwide.

We do not rely on passive phone calls; we physically audit fabrication bays, verify raw material mill delivery receipts, monitor machining queues, verify sub-contractor NDT progress, and establish realistic 4-week lookahead recovery schedules to safeguard your critical project delivery milestones.

Key Inspection Stages & Quality Points

Stage 01

Sub-Order Raw Material Mill Tracking

Auditing purchase order placements for heavy plates, forgings, casting ingots, and monitoring steel mill roll dates.

Stage 02

Foundry & Forge Shop Progress Audits

Physical verification of pattern making, casting pours, forging operations, and rough machining schedules.

Stage 03

Fabrication & Assembly Shop Progress Audits

Tracking cutting, rolling, fit-up, welding, and internal assembly progress against the master project schedule (L3/L4).

Stage 04

Special Process & Sub-Contractor Scheduling

Auditing third-party heat treatment (PWHT), blast painting, and NDT laboratory queue times.

Stage 05

Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) Readiness

Verifying test bench setup, calibrated instrument availability, and client witness availability to prevent testing delays.

Stage 06

Packaging, Clearance & Logistics Expediting

Tracking crate construction, shipping documentation clearances, export licenses, and vessel booking confirmations.

Inspection Deliverables & Reports

  • ✓ Field Expediting Reports (FER): Real-time, fact-checked progress reports with annotated shop-floor photographs and progress percentage audits.
  • ✓ Critical Bottleneck Identification & Recovery Plan: Detailed root cause analysis of delays with actionable recovery recommendations.
  • ✓ Sub-Supplier Procurement Status Matrix: End-to-end tracking log of all critical long-lead items and raw material consignments.
  • ✓ 4-Week Lookahead Schedule Audits: Rolling schedule projections cross-referenced against realistic shop production throughput.

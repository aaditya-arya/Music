Technical Scope & Methodology

Procuring critical engineering components, specialized valves, forged flanges, turbine skids, and electrical switchgear from overseas suppliers exposes project developers to significant quality, logistical, and compliance risks. Akshar Engineering Services bridges the geographic gap by providing trusted technical surveillance at foreign vendor facilities.

We act as your local eyes and ears across Asia, Europe, and the Middle East, conducting supplier quality audits, witnessing in-process manufacturing stages, presiding over Factory Acceptance Tests (FAT), and authorizing commercial Letter of Credit (LC) documentation clearances.

Key Inspection Stages & Quality Points

Stage 01

Pre-Award Overseas Facility Capability Audit

Auditing foreign manufacturer plant infrastructure, international certifications, and raw material supplier networks.

Stage 02

Kick-Off Meeting (KOM) & QAP Alignment

Clarifying client project technical specifications (PTS), hold points, and inspection protocols with foreign vendor engineering teams.

Stage 03

In-Process Hold Point Witness Surveillance

Witnessing material melting, forging, shell rolling, welding, and NDE at overseas manufacturing workshops.

Stage 04

Factory Acceptance Test (FAT) Protocol Witness

Witnessing electrical functional runs, mechanical load tests, hydrostatic testing, and control logic simulations.

Stage 05

Export Packaging & Sea-Worthy Stuffing

Auditing international export crating, vapor corrosion inhibitors (VCI), container lashing, and moisture protection.

Stage 06

Letter of Credit (LC) Quality Clearance

Issuing stamped inspection release certificates required for bank document negotiation and commercial payment release.

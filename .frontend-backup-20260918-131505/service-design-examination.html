Engineering Design Examination

Independent engineering verification of pressure vessel calculations, nozzle reinforcement analysis, and fabrication drawing appraisals.

Request This Inspection

Technical Scope & Methodology

Before metal is cut or welding begins on critical process equipment, rigorous design verification is necessary to guarantee statutory safety and design compliance. Akshar Engineering Services provides independent design appraisal services for pressure vessels, shell-and-tube heat exchangers, storage tanks, and industrial piping.

Our senior design examiners independently review mechanical calculation packages, wind and seismic loadings, nozzle reinforcement calculations, flange ratings, and Finite Element Analysis (FEA) reports against ASME Section VIII, PD 5500, EN 13445, and TEMA standards, issuing formal Design Appraisal Certificates (DAC).

Key Inspection Stages & Quality Points

Stage 01

Design Code & Specification Verification

Reviewing process design conditions: design pressure, design temperature, corrosion allowances, and statutory design codes.

Stage 02

Pressure Boundary Shell & Head Calculations

Independent re-calculation of required shell thicknesses, ellipsoidal/torispherical head thicknesses, and external vacuum ratings.

Stage 03

Nozzle Reinforcement & External Load Analysis

Verifying opening reinforcement area replacement per UG-37, WRC 107/297 local stress analysis, and piping loads.

Stage 04

Heat Exchanger Tubesheet & TEMA Calculations

Appraising fixed/floating tubesheet thicknesses, tube-to-tubesheet joint ratings, and baffle spacing calculations.

Stage 05

Structural Support, Saddle & Skirt Stress Analysis

Auditing Zick saddle stress calculations for horizontal vessels and skirt/base ring anchoring for tall vertical columns.

Stage 06

Fabrication GA & Detail Shop Drawing Review

Cross-checking detail shop fabrication drawings, weld joint details, nozzle orientation schedules, and bill of materials.

Applicable Statutory Codes & Standards

ASME Sec VIII Div 1 & Div 2 PD 5500 Unfired Pressure Vessels BS EN 13445 Pressure Vessels TEMA Standards for Heat Exchangers API 650 Welded Storage Tanks API 620 Low-Pressure Tanks

Inspection Deliverables & Reports

  • ✓ Formal Design Appraisal Certificate (DAC): Official statutory verification certificate confirming design compliance with international pressure codes.
  • ✓ Mechanical Calculation Review Matrix: Detailed itemized review notes comparing fabricator calculations with independent audit results.
  • ✓ Redlined Fabrication Drawings & Comment Log: Stamped drawing approvals with highlighted redline corrections for shop release.
  • ✓ Code Exemption & Technical Query Resolution: Documented technical justifications for code queries, waivers, and equivalent standard approvals.

Technical Overview & Execution Scope

Welding integrity is the foundation of pressure equipment, structural integrity, and industrial piping. Akshar Engineering Services deploys certified CSWIP 3.1 and AWS CWI welding engineers to oversee and qualify welding procedures and personnel across diverse alloy systems including carbon steel, austenitic stainless steel, duplex, super duplex, and nickel alloys.

We review Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS), witness Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) coupon welding, supervise destructive mechanical testing at accredited laboratories, and certify Welder Performance Qualifications (WPQ) according to ASME Section IX, AWS D1.1, and ISO 15614 standards.

Key Inspection Stages & Quality Points

Stage 01

WPS Preparation & Code Compliance Vetting

Reviewing essential, non-essential, and supplementary essential variables per applicable construction codes.

Stage 02

PQR Test Coupon Witness & Parameter Logging

On-site monitoring of preheat, interpass temperature, travel speed, heat input (kJ/mm), and shielding gas flow rate.

Stage 03

Destructive Mechanical Testing Witness

Supervising tensile tests, root/face/side bend tests, Charpy V-notch impact tests at sub-zero temperatures, and hardness surveys.

Stage 04

Metallurgical Macro-Etch & Microstructure Review

Evaluating weld penetration, fusion, heat-affected zone (HAZ) grain structure, and ferrite phase content in duplex steels.

Stage 05

Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) Testing

Conducting welder qualification tests in 1G through 6GR positions and issuing stamped Welder Qualification Cards.

Stage 06

Consumable Storage & Oven Baking Surveillance

Monitoring low-hydrogen electrode holding ovens, flux storage conditions, batch test certificates, and wire traceability.

Applicable Statutory Codes & Standards

ASME Section IX Welding & Brazing AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Steel AWS D1.6 Structural Stainless Steel ISO 15614-1 / ISO 9606-1 Standards API 1104 Pipeline Welding BS EN 287 / ISO 3834

Inspection Deliverables & Reports

  • ✓ WPS & PQR Endorsement Certificates: Formal third-party approval and stamping of welding procedure specifications and qualification records.
  • ✓ Welder Performance Qualification (WPQ) Cards: Laminated photo-ID welder certification cards with qualified thickness and positional range limits.
  • ✓ Destructive Mechanical Test Witness Reports: Validated lab test reports documenting tensile, impact, bend, and hardness test results.
  • ✓ Weld Joint Traceability Maps & Heat Input Logs: Field logs documenting heat input calculations and interpass temperature controls.
